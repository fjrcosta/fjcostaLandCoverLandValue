Deep Learning for Urban Land Cover & Land Value
with Mobility and Municipal Economics Analysis

Integrated visualization of urban land-cover classification produced with DINOv2 ViT-L/14 + LoRA and median unit land-value estimates produced with TabPFN v2, combined with the urban transport network, one-hour commute reachability and municipal land economics.

Londrina–Maringá Urban System, Paraná, Brazil

Interactive Analytical Map

Urban land value, land cover, the transport network, and municipal economics can be selected and overlaid freely.

Multimodal Commute Reachability (Londrina)

From any location selected on the map — typically a bare-soil or row-crop patch considered for residential rezoning — the portal computes which parts of the city can be reached within a one-hour one-way commute, following Bertaud's one-hour travel-time budget. Overlaying the reachable area with industrial land cover, used here as a simplified proxy for employment centres, indicates whether new residential land would remain connected to the urban labour market. Patches beyond reach are dimmed in every active layer, so the predicted unit land values within the one-hour commute can be read in the same view.
